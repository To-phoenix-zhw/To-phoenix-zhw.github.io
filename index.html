Selected Publications

  1. MolEM: a unified generative framework for molecular graphs and sequential orders. Hanwen Zhang. Briefings in Bioinformatics, 2025. SCI Q2, CCF-B.

    Proposes a variational EM framework that jointly learns 3D molecular graphs and generation orders, improving binding affinity and conformational plausibility in target-based molecular design.

  2. Policy-based active learning for efficient molecular identification. Hanwen Zhang. Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling, 2026. SCI Q2.

    Models molecular candidate screening as a Markov decision process and trains a policy network to optimize discovery efficiency under constrained experimental budgets.

  3. ProtoDiff: Prototypical diffusion model for few-shot molecular image generation. Hanwen Zhang and collaborators. IEEE/ACM Transactions on Computational Biology and Bioinformatics, 2025. SCI Q3, CCF-B.
